# Ferns & Petals Sales Analysis Dashboard

## Overview
This project presents an interactive Microsoft Excel dashboard built to analyse the Ferns & Petals sales dataset. The dashboard provides insights into sales performance, customer spending, product performance, delivery metrics, and order distribution through interactive visualisations.

## Key Metrics
- Total Revenue
- Total Orders
- Average Order Spending
- Average Delivery Time

---

## Dashboard Features
- Revenue by Month
- Revenue by Occasion
- Revenue by Hour
- Top 5 Products by Revenue
- Category-wise Revenue
- Top 10 Cities by Orders
- Interactive Slicers for Month and Date Filtering

---

## Business Questions Solved
- What is the total revenue generated?
- Which products contribute the highest revenue?
- Which cities generate the most orders?
- How does revenue vary across months and occasions?
- What is the average customer spending?
- What is the average delivery time?
- How does revenue vary throughout the day?
